# Brochure assets and font licences

The generator loads these unmodified TrueType files from this directory. It does not download fonts or depend on a machine-specific font installation.

## Provenance and verification

Font name-table records 0, 5, 13 and 14 were inspected to verify the copyright, version and licence of these specific files. The destination photographs have separate licences recorded in `public/images/latin-america/CREDITS.txt` and on the brochure's final page.
